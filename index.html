Fuel rates
New fuel entries fill these in automatically and you can change them per entry. Past entries keep the prices they were saved with.
Pay rates
Own figure, not tied to the driver deposit. For a driver's second trip the same day the deposit is paid again and the balance held is reduced by this amount.
Each plate is tied to one driver. Picking the plate on the Enter tab fills the driver automatically.
Expense reasons
Reasons you can pick for Other expense when filling a trip entry. Changes save straight away.
Tap ✎ to rename a reason. Reports and entries then show the new name, and past entries are not edited. Removing a reason does not change past entries either.
